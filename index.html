How this is calculated · sources

Resting energy: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age (years), then +5 for the male equation or −161 for the female equation. Mifflin–St Jeor study.

Daily maintenance: resting energy × an approximate activity factor (1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, or 1.9). These factors are separate from the original equation. Exercise is already included; do not add workout calories again. Activity factors and goal adjustments.

Goal settings: fat loss starts at −500 kcal/day; muscle gain starts at +300 kcal/day; maintenance has no adjustment. These are starting settings, not a prediction of weight or muscle change. NHS weight-gain guidance.

Scope: adult estimates, not measured metabolism. Review your average weight trend over 2–4 weeks and adjust. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, medical conditions and hormone therapy may need an individual approach. Fat-loss targets are withheld for BMI below 18.5. The app reduces the deficit at 1,200 kcal (female) or 1,500 kcal (male); these are conservative software limits, not a guarantee of adequate nutrition. Weight-management guideline.

Other targets: the protein range is for resistance training (research). Water is a general adult total from food and all beverages, not an amount to drink on top of food (reference). Weekly movement follows CDC guidance. The walking goal retains your original weight-based rule; it is not a validated requirement. The old weekly weight-loss prediction has been removed.
